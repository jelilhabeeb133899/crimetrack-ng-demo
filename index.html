A student demonstration of a web-based crime reporting and tracking system, designed for mobile and desktop screens.

How it works

Submit incident details → receive a reference number → check the demonstration status. In a real system, reports must be reviewed by authorised personnel.

Demonstration only: This prototype stores sample reports only in this browser. Do not enter real names, addresses, evidence or confidential police information. It is not connected to the Nigeria Police Force and is not an emergency service.
